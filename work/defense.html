Defense equity event study

Do defense stocks beat the market when conflicts break out?

Empirical researchDefense equitiesDo defense stocks beat the market in conflicts? No reliable evidence (p = 0.516)January–June 2026
Type
Empirical finance research, solo
When
January–June 2026
Tools
R
Result
p = 0.516

The question

The story is intuitive: conflict raises expectations of military spending, so defense contractors should outperform. I wanted to know whether the data actually supports that.

Method

  • Sample: 7 defense contractors, compared against the S&P 500
  • Events: 3 geopolitical conflicts
  • Window: ±90 days around each event
  • Tests: hypothesis tests on pre- versus post-event returns, plus difference-in-differences against the market

Result

No statistically significant evidence of consistent outperformance (p = 0.516).

That doesn't mean conflicts never move defense stocks. It means that, across these events, outperformance wasn't reliable enough to trade on.

What I learned

Three claims are easy to mix up: a stock went up, a stock beat the market, and an event caused it to beat the market. A persuasive narrative can sound like all three while supporting only the first.
