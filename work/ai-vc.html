AI in Venture Capital

Does AI make venture investors better, or just more confident?

The question

Venture firms increasingly use AI to screen startups. The research on whether it actually improves their decisions is spread across finance, management and computer science journals. Before I can propose an original research question, I need to know what has been studied and where the gaps are.

My approach

Rather than running searches and reading abstracts by hand, I built a pipeline that does discovery and screening systematically. I review every conclusion myself; the pipeline only narrows the field.

Results so far

Phase 1 is complete. The first live run returned 46 papers from 2020 onward. After merging 5 duplicates, 41 were unique, and I selected 5 for an initial pilot.

Two directions look promising. These are open questions, not findings:

  • Does AI help investors make better early-stage decisions, or mainly make them more confident in automated recommendations?
  • Does explainable AI improve decisions for highly novel startups, where past data says the least?

Next

Retrieve full texts, expand to 20–50 papers, compare methods and limitations across them, and write one research-gap report to review with Professor Batebi.

Technical details

Python; Gemini API for query generation; OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar and SSRN for discovery; SQLite and JSON caching. The pipeline retries failed API calls and saves partial results rather than treating them as complete. 25 automated tests.
