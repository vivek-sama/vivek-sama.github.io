Pricing the cables behind the AI build-out

The question

Every new data center needs power, and power needs cable. Prysmian, one of the world's largest cable makers, sits between grid investment and AI infrastructure demand. We asked whether its expected earnings growth justified the price.

What I did

I built the financial model while my teammate led the pitch deck. The model covered:

  • Historical financial statements and projections through 2030
  • Revenue, operating margin and earnings forecasts
  • Bear, base and bull scenarios with different exit multiples
  • Balance-sheet and cash-flow reconciliation checks, which the final model passed

The valuation

Starting from €109.40 (October 16, 2026), our base case reached a 2030 target of €143.14, about 7.6% a year including dividends.

Limits

We valued Prysmian on a standalone basis. A previously discussed acquisition of Atkore was not built into the earnings, financing or integration assumptions. I flagged that limitation in our model review, and the next version would model the deal.
